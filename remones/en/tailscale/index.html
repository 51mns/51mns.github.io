Use it away from home (Tailscale)

Remones connects your iPhone directly to your Mac. To use it away from home, put both on the same VPN. This page explains how to do that with Tailscale.

On this page

  1. How it works
  2. Setting it up
  3. Adding a Mac manually
  4. Staying safe

How it works

Tailscale is a VPN service that connects your own devices to each other. When your iPhone and Mac join the same Tailscale network (a tailnet), your Mac becomes reachable from anywhere.

Remones uses your local network when you’re nearby, and the Tailscale route when the local one can’t be reached. Nothing goes through Remones servers. Tailscale is optional; other VPNs work too.

Setting it up

  1. Install Tailscale on both

    Install Tailscale on your iPhone (or iPad) and on your Mac. Download Tailscale

  2. Sign in to the same account

    Sign in to the same Tailscale account on both devices so they join the same tailnet, and make sure Tailscale is on for both.

  3. Connect once on the same Wi-Fi

    With Tailscale on for the Mac, connect to it once from Remones while you’re on the same Wi-Fi. Your iPhone then learns the Mac’s Tailscale address.

  4. Open it while away

    Away from home, turn on Tailscale on your iPhone and open your Mac from Remones’ Home as usual.

For more on Tailscale itself, see the Tailscale documentation.

Adding a Mac manually

If you can’t connect on the same Wi-Fi first, add the Mac by its Tailscale address. Tap “+” on Home and enter one of these as the address:

  • The Mac’s Tailscale name (MagicDNS name). The short name and the full name both work.
  • The Mac’s Tailscale address, beginning with 100.

The Tailscale app on your Mac shows both. See MagicDNS in the Tailscale docs. The first connection still needs approval on the Mac.

Staying safe

Don’t expose the Mac’s port (default 7777) directly to the internet, for example with port forwarding on your router. Use a VPN such as Tailscale instead.

Connections are encrypted with TLS, and only paired devices can connect. You can review and remove paired devices in Remones Host on the Mac.
