1. Scope and approach

This policy covers the Remones iPhone/iPad app and Mac Host.

Remones connects to a Mac you own or are authorized to control, using your local network or a VPN you arrange. It requires no Remones account or developer-operated shared backend or relay.

Normal remote sessions do not send screen content, audio, input, or clipboard content to a developer-operated server.

2. Information exchanged between devices

Depending on the features you use, connected devices process and exchange:

  • Mac screen and window video, cursor information, and Mac playback audio when enabled.
  • Keyboard input, entered text, pointer/touch/trackpad actions, and focus information needed for control.
  • Device identifiers and names, connection addresses, authentication information, connection status, capabilities, build information, and battery status.
  • Application names, identifiers and icons, running status, and window titles and geometry used to select remote applications and views.
  • Text and URLs when clipboard synchronization is enabled. In versions supporting multiple Macs, switching the active computer with synchronization enabled may send copied text to the destination Mac.
  • Search queries and results when file search is allowed on the Mac. Search uses filename metadata on the Mac. Results include names, parent-folder names, types, and temporary identifiers for opening items. Search results do not transfer file contents or full paths, although opened content can appear in the screen stream.

The destination Mac and applications running on it may process or retain received input and clipboard content.

The reviewed implementation contains iPhone/iPad microphone and camera forwarding code, but the normal Mac Host configuration disables these capabilities. Enabling them in a release requires reviewing this policy and permission descriptions.

3. Information stored locally

Remones stores information on your devices to support reconnection and restore settings:

  • Keychain: client identity, paired-device information, and shared secrets. The Mac also stores approved-device information.
  • Preferences: display, input, audio and clipboard settings, favorites, recent remote targets, and workspace names, targets and layouts.
  • Application files: connected Macs’ application catalogs, names, identifiers, usage timestamps and related metadata, and application icons.
  • Mac recovery records: identifiers, window geometry, settings and operation states needed to restore windows or system settings.
  • Diagnostics: local connection, error and timing logs.

In the reviewed version, session screen thumbnails are held in memory; no Remones code persisting those thumbnails as image files was identified.

OS backups, system clipboard sharing, and storage by other applications follow their respective settings and policies.

4. Purchases and external services

Remones Plus uses Apple StoreKit for purchases, restoration and subscription status. The app processes product and transaction information, expiration dates and renewal status. Remones does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password.

Apple’s payment, distribution and diagnostic processing is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Downloading the Mac Host or viewing documents through GitHub or GitHub Pages exposes access information, such as your IP address, to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

VPN use is optional. Your chosen VPN provider and network administrator may process information under their own terms.

5. Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

No advertising, third-party usage analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK was identified in the reviewed app implementation. Remones has no proprietary advertising tracking or automatic local-log upload feature.

Diagnostic sharing through the OS or Apple services follows your settings and Apple’s mechanisms.

6. Choices, retention and deletion

Clipboard synchronization is off by default. Disable synchronization, audio or Picture in Picture when you do not want those features. Picture in Picture may continue displaying Mac video or playing audio after you leave the main view.

The computer-removal feature removes the corresponding pairing and cached application information. Recent items and workspaces may require separate removal. Revoke the approved client on the Mac as appropriate.

Local records may remain until removed or replaced. Log retention is managed by the OS and relevant logging mechanism. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily erase Keychain entries, Mac-side data, backups or Apple purchase records. The developer cannot remotely delete data stored on your devices.

7. Security

Connections use TLS. During initial pairing, compare the six-digit codes on both devices and approve only the intended connection. Encryption alone does not authenticate the initial peer.

8. Contact and changes

If you voluntarily provide contact details, descriptions or logs for support, they will be used for responding and investigating the issue and retained only as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Do not send secret keys or unnecessary personal information.

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
