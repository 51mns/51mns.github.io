FAQ

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 18 or later, and a Mac with macOS 15 or later running the Mac app, Remones Host.

Does it work with Windows or Android?

Remones currently supports controlling a Mac from an iPhone or iPad.

Do I need an account?

No. Just pair your iPhone with your Mac.

Is my screen or input sent anywhere?

It goes directly between your iPhone and your Mac, never to a developer server.

Is the connection encrypted?

Yes, connections use TLS. When you connect for the first time, compare the six-digit code on your iPhone and Mac to make sure it’s the right Mac before approving.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, with a VPN such as Tailscale. See Use it away from home.

Can I add more than one Mac?

Yes. Your Macs appear side by side on Home and you can switch between them.

Can two iPhones control the same Mac at once?

One device controls a Mac at a time. If another device is connected, close it there and try again.

The screen is black

Screen Recording is probably not allowed on the Mac. Check Permissions, then quit and reopen the Mac app.

I can see the screen but can’t control it

Allow Accessibility for Remones Host on the Mac.

My iPhone can’t find the Mac

Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi and that Remones is on under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network on your iPhone. See Troubleshooting for more.

iOS keeps asking before every paste

To send text copied on your iPhone, tap Paste under Input in the session menu. That way iOS doesn’t need to ask.

How do I remove a Mac?

On your iPhone, use the “…” menu of the Mac on Home. Files and apps on the Mac are not deleted. You can also remove the approved device in the Mac app.

What is Remones Plus?

An optional subscription that lets you choose the app icon and in-app accent colour. Standard and Lemon are free. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store. Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Report a problem

If something doesn’t work, such as connecting, please include the following. It helps find the cause much faster.

  1. Copy the connection diagnostic

    When a connection fails, tap “Copy connection diagnostic”. A one-line diagnostic is copied to your iPhone’s clipboard.

    It contains only the app build, the kind of failure and how far the connection got. It does not include device names, IP addresses, host names, keys or pairing codes.

  2. Describe what happened

    When it happened and what you were doing, your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, your Mac’s macOS version, and whether you were on the same Wi-Fi or on Tailscale.

  3. Send it

    A contact address is being prepared. If you’re testing through TestFlight, please send feedback from the TestFlight app.

Please don’t send pairing keys, passwords or other secrets, or personal information that isn’t needed.
