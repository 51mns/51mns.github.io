Get started

Setting up Remones for the first time. It goes smoothly with your iPhone or iPad and your Mac nearby, on the same Wi-Fi.

Steps

  1. 1. Install the Mac app
  2. 2. Allow permissions
  3. 3. Add your Mac
  4. 4. Approve on the Mac
  5. 5. Open

1. Install the Mac app

On the Mac you want to control, open this address:

51mns.github.io/remones/en/mac/

Download Remones Host, move it to the Applications folder and open it. See installing the Mac app for details.

2. Allow permissions

On the Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and allow these two for Remones Host:

  • Screen & System Audio Recording — needed to show the Mac’s screen on your iPhone.
  • Accessibility — needed to control the Mac from your iPhone. Not required if you only want to view.

Then quit Remones Host and open it again.

3. Add your Mac

Open Remones on your iPhone or iPad and tap “+” on Home. There are three ways to add a Mac.

Nearby Macs

Macs on the same network appear automatically. Just pick yours.

Enter an address

Enter the Mac’s IP address or host name. You can usually leave the port empty (the default is 7777).

Tailscale

Enter the Mac’s Tailscale name or its address beginning with 100. Learn more

The first time Remones looks for Macs, iOS asks whether it may find devices on your local network. Choose Allow. To change it later, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network on your iPhone and turn on Remones.

4. Approve on the Mac

Your iPhone shows a pairing code, and the Mac shows a request. Check that the codes match, then approve.

  • Always Allow — saves this iPhone so it can connect without approval next time.
  • Approve Once — allows this connection only.
  • Reject — declines. Use this if you don’t recognise the request.

5. Open

When your Mac shows as online on Home, tap Open. You’re all set.

Tips for your first session

  • The session menu switches between Display, Input and Audio.
  • Open the Shelf to jump to other windows, apps and Macs.
  • Going back to Home keeps the Mac connected.
  • To send text copied on your iPhone, use Paste in Input.

Having trouble? See Troubleshooting or Support.
